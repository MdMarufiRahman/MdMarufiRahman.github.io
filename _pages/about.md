### Welcome!
<p align="justify">
I hold a Ph.D. in computer science and specialize in the intersection of computer vision, motion estimation, and machine learning systems. My research focuses on building robust image- and video-analysis pipelines, ranging from medical imaging—including endoscopy and colonoscopy—to robotics and navigation. By combining deep learning, GPU acceleration, and geometric vision, I aim to solve problems in which precision and reliability directly affect human safety. I pursued this Ph.D. to bridge fundamental research and real-world systems. My work has resulted in peer-reviewed publications and collaborations with researchers in academia and industry.
</p>
