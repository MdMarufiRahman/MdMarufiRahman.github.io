### Welcome!
<p align="justify">
I hold a Ph.D. in computer science and specialize at the intersection of computer vision, motion estimation, and machine learning systems.
My research centers on building robust image and video analysis pipelines — from medical imaging (endoscopy and colonoscopy) to robotics and navigation. By combining deep learning, GPU acceleration, and geometric vision, I aim to solve problems where precision and reliability directly impact human safety.
I pursued this PhD to bridge fundamental research and real-world systems. My work has led to peer-reviewed publications and collaborations across labs and industry.
</p>
